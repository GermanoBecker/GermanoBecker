### 🔬 Research Project

**Pathological Effects of Sea Air on Carbon Steel Structures**
*(Efeitos Patológicos da Maresia em Estruturas de Aço Carbono)*

Coastal regions expose metal structures to a salt-laden atmosphere (sea air), which speeds up
the corrosion of carbon steel. This project studies how sea air affects carbon steel structures,
which pathologies it causes (such as rust, pitting and loss of thickness) and which methods can
help prevent or reduce the damage.
